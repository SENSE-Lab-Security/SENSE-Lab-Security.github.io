Our Mission

The SENSE Lab at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou) advances the science and engineering of secure and privacy-preserving sensing and computation. We protect emerging technologies such as embodied AI, the Internet of Things, and ubiquitous computing by investigating both the software and hardware stacks of computer systems. Contact us at yanlong@hkust-gz.edu.cn.
